# MARS Cashflow (Web Edition)

A modern, responsive React & TypeScript web application ported from the MARS Cashflow Android project.

## Overview
MARS Cashflow connects Landlords, Caretakers, and Tenants into a single, unified real estate cashflow ledger designed specifically for the Uganda property ecosystem.

## Key Features Ported
- **Multi-Role Workspaces**: Instant switching between Landlord (Owner), Caretaker (Manager), Tenant (Occupant), and Contractor (Service Provider) views.
- **Rent Collections & Digital Receipts**: Fast recording of payments via MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Bank EFT, and Cash, generating cryptographically verified digital receipts with printing and sharing capabilities.
- **Arrears Management & SMS Reminders**: Real-time tenant payment status tracking, debt aging, and 1-click broadcast reminder dispatches.
- **Operating Expense Ledger & OCR Scanner**: Logging of maintenance, utility tokens (Umeme Yaka, NWSC water), and wages with simulated OCR receipt scanning.
- **Maintenance & Work Orders**: Ticket logging with priority matrix, status workflow (Pending, In Progress, Resolved), and recurring preventive servicing schedules.
- **Service Provider Directory**: Vetted database of plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and artisans with ratings and direct call actions.
- **Financial Analytics & Trends**: Interactive inflow vs outflow trend visualizations with Recharts and net cashflow calculations.
- **Budget Planner & PDF Statements**: Expense caps vs actual variance control and printable monthly financial audit statements.
- **Uganda Tenancy Act (2022) Compliance & FAQs**: Built-in regulatory guidance for notices, deposits, and rent in UGX.
